1. Local Storage and Game Progress

Lazer Teaser does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

To provide a seamless gameplay experience, the app utilizes your device's local storage solely to save your game progression (such as the highest level reached). This allows you to close the app and resume your game later without losing your progress.

  • This data is stored locally on your own mobile device.
  • This data is never uploaded to an external server, shared with third parties, or monitored by me.
  • Clearing the app's cache or uninstalling the app will permanently delete this locally stored progress.

2. Device Permissions

The app does not require or request any sensitive device permissions (such as access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise GPS location).

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics tools, advertisement networks, cloud databases, or tracking frameworks that collect data or monitor user behaviour.

4. Security

Because your progress remains strictly on your personal device and no data is transmitted over the internet, your information is completely secure within your device's local environment.

5. Children's Privacy

Because this app does not collect or transmit any data externally, it is inherently safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13.
